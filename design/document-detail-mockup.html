Your Second Brain is a digital archive of your most valuable ideas, insights, and knowledge. It's a system that helps you capture, organize, and retrieve information so you can focus on what matters most: creative work.

This is the core thesis of the book. It's not just about saving things—it's about freeing up cognitive resources for actual thinking.

Nov 14, 2024 Location 142

CODE stands for Capture, Organize, Distill, Express. These four steps form the core methodology for building a second brain that actually works.

Nov 14, 2024 Location 298

Don't just capture information—capture what resonates. The goal isn't to create a comprehensive archive of everything you encounter. It's to save the ideas that spark something in you, that you find yourself thinking about later.

Resonance-based capture. This is different from trying to save "important" things—it's about emotional/intellectual reaction.

Nov 15, 2024 Location 456

PARA stands for Projects, Areas, Resources, and Archives. It's a universal system for organizing any type of digital information across any platform.

Nov 16, 2024 Location 612

Progressive Summarization is the technique of taking notes on your notes, in layers. Each pass makes the most important points more visible without losing the original context.

Key technique! Layer 1: bold key passages. Layer 2: highlight the bold. Layer 3: executive summary. Do this progressively as you use the note, not all at once.

Nov 17, 2024 Location 789

The most important thing is to start creating. Your Second Brain is not a library to visit occasionally—it's a factory for producing creative output.

Nov 18, 2024 Location 1024

Intermediate Packets are reusable building blocks of work: notes, outlines, drafts, checklists, templates. When you break your work into these packets, you can mix and match them to create new outputs much faster.

This concept connects to atomic design in software. Small, composable units. Apply to writing, presentations, research.
